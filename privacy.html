Privacy policy

This OAuth client is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

  • The client reads and writes files in the owner's Google Drive to sync them with the owner's private server.
  • No data is collected from other people.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's server.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of Google user data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
